Chapter IX – The Gifts of the Fates

Morning came quietly to the tavern after the dangers of the night before. The owner welcomed Pip and Luna with a generous breakfast and enough provisions to see them safely on their way: dry mushroom cakes, full water skins, hard bread, smoked lake fish, and salt. Before they left, he warned them not to go wandering into danger again, asking only that they return safely.

On the road, the Witch and the Music Weaver appeared once more. The Witch offered a strange warning: “The birds that sing do not always fly. Watch close when the red breasts turn to their sides.” Then, with a knowing smile, she presented the travelers with magical dice, gifts from the Fates. Luna rolled first and received the Hag’s Walking Hut, a sanctuary that could appear whenever she needed shelter. Pip’s roll granted him the Black Candle of Borrowed Midnight, a candle whose dark flame seemed to carry secrets of its own.

Later, the Mayor waited for them inside a beautiful library, where he spoke at length about its magnificent shelves. An old cartographer had corrected the maps and revealed two possible roads toward Archivon: a fast but dangerous route through the dark old forest, or a longer river path that offered daylight, water, and a little more safety. Pip, meanwhile, became completely absorbed in tracing the connections between one book and another, as though the library itself were whispering clues.

In the end, Pip and Luna chose the river. It would take longer, but the daylight, the water, and the safer road seemed worth the time. With new gifts from the Fates and an uncertain path ahead, the two companions continued toward Archivon, carrying with them more questions than answers.
